Account deletion

Delete your AxomQuest account

Last updated: 29 October 2026

AxomQuest (formerly Mission Khaki) allows you to delete your account and associated cloud data at any time, whether you use the free tier or subscribe to AxomQuest Pro. You can delete your account from within the app or request deletion via email without reinstalling the app.

On this page
  1. Delete in the app
  2. Request deletion without the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. Device-local data
  5. Minimal security records retained

1. Delete in the app

In the AxomQuest app (or in previous Mission Khaki versions under Account settings):

  1. Open the app and navigate to Account (or Account settings).
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. Re-authenticate with your sign-in method (Google Sign-In or your account email) to verify account ownership.
  4. Review the confirmation notice, type DELETE when prompted, and confirm.

Upon successful verification, the app deletes your cloud identity and signs you out. If a network interruption occurs, the app reports an incomplete status so you can retry safely.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the app, switched devices, or cannot access your phone, you do not need to reinstall AxomQuest:

  • Send an email to contact.rupjyoti26@gmail.com with the subject AxomQuest Account Deletion Request.
  • Include the email address associated with your AxomQuest account (or your legacy sign-in phone number if you previously registered under Mission Khaki).
  • State clearly that you request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Security Notice: Never send your password, verification codes, OTPs, UPI/bank details, or purchase tokens to our support team. We will never ask for them.

We verify account ownership before deleting data, ask for clarification if needed, and confirm completion by email. Verified external requests are typically processed within 30 days. Submitting a request is not completed deletion until ownership verification succeeds.

3. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your Firebase Authentication identity (your account credentials and identifier).
  • Any application-owned cloud profile records associated with your account UID.
  • Active server-side entitlement projections granting Pro access.

Deleting an account ends linked Pro membership on that account. A retired purchase cannot automatically unlock a newly created account through Restore.

4. Device-local data

In AxomQuest, your completed mock test attempts, scores, question bookmarks, history, and user preferences are stored locally on your device, partitioned by user account.

Because this study data is stored on your device rather than a shared cloud server, deleting your cloud account does not automatically wipe local files on your physical device. To remove local study data from your device:

  • Go to your phone’s Android Settings → Apps → AxomQuest → Storage & cache → Clear storage; or
  • Uninstall the AxomQuest application from your device.

5. Minimal security records retained

To prevent fraud, avoid purchase replay, and meet tax and legal integrity obligations, minimal pseudonymous records may be retained:

  • Purchase Integrity Records: For paid accounts, we retain one-way hashed purchase-token fingerprints with a retired flag. These records contain no account UID, name, email address, phone number, raw purchase token, card number, or banking details.
  • Deletion Status Records: A minimal deletion-status log keyed to the former pseudonymous identifier records completion time or any incomplete cleanup stage, blocking stale sessions from resurrecting deleted records and permitting safe cleanup retries.

These minimal records are retained strictly for security, fraud prevention, and transaction verification. Google may also retain copies of transaction data in its own Google Play billing systems as governed by Google’s privacy terms.

6. Important: Google Play subscriptions

Deleting your AxomQuest account or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel your Google Play recurring subscription or issue a refund.

Google Play manages recurring billing independently. If you have an active AxomQuest Pro monthly subscription, you must cancel it in Google Play before deleting your account to prevent future billing:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your Profile icon at the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select AxomQuest from your list of subscriptions.
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the on-screen instructions.

Subscription payments are generally non-refundable upon cancellation. Cancelling stops future renewals, and Pro access continues until the end of the current paid billing period, subject to Google Play's policies and applicable law.
