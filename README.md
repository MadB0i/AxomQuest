# AxomQuest — Official Website

A static marketing and legal website for **AxomQuest**, an Android competitive-exam preparation app for Assam Police and ADRE Grade 3 & 4 aspirants. Plain HTML/CSS/JS — **no build step, no npm, no bundler** — deploys directly to GitHub Pages.

- **Live site:** https://madb0i.github.io/AxomQuest/
- **Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rupjyoti.missionkhaki

## About the app

**AxomQuest** is an Android app for competitive examination preparation. It features 100+ online mock tests, 3,000+ questions, 50+ study materials & PDF notes (12 free on free tier), and previous year question papers (PYQs) with authentic negative marking, section-wise score analytics, and instant English / Hindi / Assamese question translation during tests.

- **Free Tier:** 30 full-length online mocks (Mocks 001–030) and Daily 10 practice are completely free (ad-supported).
- **AxomQuest Pro:** An optional monthly auto-renewing subscription at ₹99/month (for India) through Google Play Billing unlocking 100+ total mocks, 50+ study material PDFs, and previous year question papers (PYQs) with an ad-free experience.
- **Historical Lifetime Rights:** Existing users with historical one-time purchases (`mission_khaki_one_time_pro`) retain full access to premium mocks without paying a subscription.

> **Independent Developer Disclosure:** AxomQuest is an independent, privately developed app. The site clearly states that it is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government or defence organisation**.
