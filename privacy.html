Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

AxomQuest is an independent, privately developed application. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Indian Army, the Ministry of Defence, the Assam Police or any state police force, the Staff Selection Commission, UPSC, SLPRB, or any government body. We operate the application and are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

2. What we collect and where it is stored

AxomQuest is designed to collect only the minimum data required to run your account, deliver mock tests, and manage Pro access.

Data Category What is Included Where It Is Stored
Account Information Your name, email address, and user ID from your chosen sign-in method (Google Sign-In or Email/Password). Existing accounts with previously verified phone numbers remain supported. Firebase Authentication (Google)
Study Attempts & Scores Completed mock attempts, section scores, answers, bookmarks, and preferences. Notice: Your test attempts and scores are saved directly on your phone, not on our cloud servers. They do not automatically sync across separate devices. Stored locally on your phone
Online Mock Content Delivering online mock tests and Daily 10 questions to the app, and verifying your access tier. Cloud Firestore (Google)
Subscriptions & Purchases Purchase confirmation and order reference from Google Play to activate your Pro subscription. Google Play Billing, with secure server verification
Diagnostics & Advertising Basic crash diagnostics, performance reports, and advertising signals (where advertising is active and you have consented). Processed on-device and via Google services
  • Sign-in Security: When you sign in with Google or Email, your password is handled directly by the authentication service and is never visible to or stored by us.
  • Financial Privacy: We never collect, see, or store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or net-banking credentials. All payments are handled by Google Play.

3. Payments & subscription verification

All subscription purchases in AxomQuest are processed through Google Play Billing on Google’s secure payment infrastructure.

  • AxomQuest Pro Monthly Subscription: AxomQuest Pro is an optional monthly subscription priced at ₹99/month for India. Google Play displays the final price (including applicable taxes) before you confirm. The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel it.
  • Upcoming Subscription Verification (Cloudflare): To protect purchases and verify subscriptions securely, an upcoming backend verification service (powered by Cloudflare) checks purchase tokens directly with Google Play and stores encrypted verification receipts. Note: This verification service is currently being tested and will become operational when the developer enables live subscription purchases.
  • Historical Lifetime Purchases: Users who previously purchased the historical one-time "Mission Khaki Lifetime Pro" package retain full access to premium mock tests without paying a monthly subscription.
  • Refund Terms: Subscription payments are generally non-refundable upon cancellation. Cancelling stops future renewals, and Pro access continues until the end of the current paid billing period, subject to Google Play's policies and applicable law.

4. Advertising and consent

Core practice features (Mocks 001–030 and Daily 10) are free to all users and may be supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob.

  • Current Status: In the current app release candidate, live advertising requests are disabled. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) is integrated into the app to manage consent choices in eligible regions when ads are active.
  • Privacy Choices: Where required, you can choose between personalised and non-personalised ads, or manage your consent settings directly within the app.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Users with an active Pro subscription or verified historical lifetime ownership practice ad-free. Timed tests are never interrupted by ads.

5. How we use data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and maintain the app:

  • To log you into your account and keep you signed in.
  • To deliver practice questions and score your tests accurately.
  • To verify your Pro subscription or historical lifetime access.
  • To process account deletion requests.
  • To fix bugs, diagnose crashes, and keep the app running reliably.

We do not sell your personal data, do not use your study scores for advertising, and do not track you across unrelated third-party apps.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We keep account and transaction records only as long as necessary to run the service and meet legal and fraud-prevention obligations.

  • Deleting in the App: In AxomQuest, open Account → Delete account (or in earlier Mission Khaki releases, Account settings → Delete account), re-confirm your sign-in, type DELETE, and confirm.
  • Requesting Deletion by Email: If you cannot access the app, email contact.rupjyoti26@gmail.com with the subject AxomQuest Account Deletion Request and provide your registered email. We verify ownership and process requests typically within 30 days. Never send passwords or financial details. Step-by-step instructions are available on the Account Deletion page.
  • What Gets Deleted: When your account is deleted, your cloud sign-in identity and account profile are permanently removed from our servers.
  • Device-Local Data: Because your mock attempts and bookmarks are saved on your phone rather than our servers, deleting your account does not wipe your phone's files. You can clear them in Android Settings (Apps → AxomQuest → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Retained Records: Minimal, non-personal transaction records (such as purchase reference numbers and deletion status) are retained to prevent fraud and satisfy legal requirements. These contain no personal names, passwords, or payment card details.
  • Important Notice on Subscriptions: Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel an active Google Play subscription. You must cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions to stop future renewals.

7. Target audience (18+)

  • AxomQuest is designed for adult competitive-examination candidates aged 18 years and older.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.
  • If you believe an account was created by a minor, contact us at contact.rupjyoti26@gmail.com and we will remove the account.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Sign-In Method: You can choose whether to sign in with Google or Email.
  • Ad Privacy: You can reset your Google Advertising ID or adjust your ad personalisation settings in your phone's Google settings.
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account at any time using the in-app option or by contacting our support email.

9. Third-party service providers

We work with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers to operate the app:

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — Secure payment and subscription processing.
  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Account authentication and online mock test delivery (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — In-app advertising and privacy consent management.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) — Upcoming secure subscription verification service (Cloudflare Privacy Policy).

10. Security practices

We use standard industry protections — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure cloud access controls — to safeguard your information. While no internet transmission is 100% immune from risk, we take reasonable, commercially accepted precautions to keep your data safe.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates or legal requirements. Material updates will be shown by the updated date above. Continued use of AxomQuest signifies acceptance of the updated terms.
