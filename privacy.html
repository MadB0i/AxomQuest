Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

AxomQuest is an independent, privately developed application. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Indian Army, the Ministry of Defence, the Assam Police or any state police force, the Staff Selection Commission, UPSC, SLPRB, or any CAPF in any way. We operate the application and are the data controller for personal data processed under this policy.

2. What we collect and where it is stored

AxomQuest is engineered to collect the minimum personal data required to authenticate your account, deliver mock tests, and securely authorize Pro entitlements.

Data Category Purpose Storage Location
Account Identity Your display name, email address, and authenticated user ID (UID) provided by your chosen sign-in provider (Google Sign-In or Email/Password). Existing Mission Khaki accounts with verified phone numbers remain supported. Firebase Authentication (Google cloud infrastructure)
Study Attempts & Scores Completed mock attempts, section-wise scores, answers, bookmarks, test history, and UI preferences. Notice: Study attempts are stored locally on your device separated by user account UID. They do not constitute a cloud backup and do not sync across separate devices. Device-local SQLite/secure storage (on your Android device)
Online Content Delivery Delivery of 108 online mock tests and Daily 10 questions, revision chunks, and minimal server-managed entitlement projections. Cloud Firestore (Google infrastructure)
Purchase & Subscription Records Product identifier, subscription status, expiration timestamps, and order references from Google Play. Planned server-side verification utilizes encrypted purchase tokens to validate access. Google Play Billing; planned Cloudflare Workers / D1 verification database
Diagnostics & Advertising Data App performance, crash diagnostics, and advertising signals (where advertising is enabled and consent has been granted). Google Mobile Ads / AdMob SDK (on-device and Google infrastructure)
  • Sign-in Providers: You can sign in using your Google account or email and password via Firebase Authentication. We only receive profile details supplied by your chosen provider (such as name and email address). Passwords are never visible to or stored by us.
  • What We Never Store: We never collect or store credit/debit card numbers, UPI IDs, net-banking passwords, or biometric information.

3. Payments & subscription verification

All purchases in AxomQuest are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing on Google’s secure payment infrastructure.

  • AxomQuest Pro Monthly Subscription: The upcoming AxomQuest Pro membership is an optional auto-renewing subscription at ₹99/month for India. Google Play displays the final price (including applicable taxes) before you confirm. We never receive or handle your financial payment instruments.
  • Planned Server Verification (Cloudflare Workers & D1): To protect user purchases and enforce server-authoritative entitlements, an upcoming verification service running on Cloudflare Workers with Cloudflare D1 storage will receive purchase tokens from Google Play, encrypt them at rest with protected server keys, and maintain minimal transaction-integrity state. Note: This verification service is currently deployed in testing and will become active when live subscription checkout is enabled by the developer.
  • Preservation of Historical Purchases: Users who previously purchased the one-time "Mission Khaki Lifetime Pro" package retain full access to premium mock tests (Mocks 031–108). Historical purchase tokens remain valid and will not be charged a monthly subscription.
  • Refund Terms: Subscription payments are generally non-refundable upon cancellation. Cancelling stops future renewals, and Pro access continues until the end of the current paid billing period, subject to Google Play's policies and applicable law.

4. Advertising and consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Core practice features (Mocks 001–030 and Daily 10) are free to all users and may be supported by advertisements served by Google AdMob.

  • Current Release Status: In the current AxomQuest release candidate, live advertising requests are disabled. The app bundles Google’s Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs in preparation for compliant consent management.
  • Consent Management: When advertising is active, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices in eligible regions, allowing users to consent to or manage personalised and non-personalised advertising.
  • Ad-Free Experience: Subscribing to AxomQuest Pro or holding a verified historical lifetime entitlement removes eligible banner and interstitial ads. Timed test attempts are never interrupted by ads.

5. How we use data

We process personal data solely for legitimate service operations:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your session.
  • Authorizing access to online mock tests and Daily 10 questions.
  • Verifying recurring subscription status and preserving historical lifetime entitlements.
  • Processing voluntary account deletion requests.
  • Diagnosing technical crashes and measuring aggregate app performance.

We do not sell personal data, do not use your study scores for marketing, and do not track your activity across unrelated third-party apps.

6. Data retention and account deletion

We retain account and transaction records only for as long as necessary to provide the service and satisfy fraud prevention and legal obligations.

  • In-App Deletion: In AxomQuest, open Account → Delete account (or in earlier Mission Khaki releases, Account settings → Delete account), re-authenticate your sign-in identity, type DELETE, and confirm.
  • External Deletion Request (Without App): If you cannot access the app, email contact.rupjyoti26@gmail.com with the subject AxomQuest Account Deletion Request, providing your registered email address. We verify ownership and process requests typically within 30 days. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or financial details. Details are available on the Account Deletion page.
  • What is Deleted: Verified deletion permanently erases your Firebase Authentication identity, application-owned cloud profile, and server-side entitlement projections.
  • Device Data: Because mock attempts and bookmarks are stored locally on your physical device, account deletion does not erase device files. To clear local data, clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.
  • Retained Records: We retain minimal pseudonymous one-way purchase-token fingerprints (with a retired flag) and deletion-status records to prevent replay abuse, fraud, and stale session resurrection. These contain no raw names, emails, phone numbers, or card details.
  • Subscription Cancellation Notice: Deleting your AxomQuest account or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel an active Google Play subscription. You must cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions to stop recurring monthly renewals.

7. Target audience and age limit (18+)

  • AxomQuest is intended and designed for adult competitive-examination aspirants aged 18 years and older.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.
  • If you believe an account was created by a minor, please contact us at contact.rupjyoti26@gmail.com and we will delete the account and associated cloud data.

8. Your privacy choices

  • Sign-in Choice: You may sign in with Google or using email and password.
  • Ad Privacy: On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation under your device’s Google settings.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to delete your account at any time using the in-app option or our external web deletion route.

9. Third-party service providers

We work with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure providers to operate AxomQuest:

  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (Google LLC) — Identity authentication and online mock test delivery (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — Payment processing and subscription management.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — Ad serving and consent management framework.
  • Cloudflare Workers & D1 (Cloudflare, Inc.) — Planned server-side verification and encrypted purchase token storage (Cloudflare Privacy Policy).

10. Security practices

We implement industry-standard technical measures — including HTTPS encryption in transit, Firebase least-privilege security rules, and server-side token encryption at rest — to protect your data. While no internet-based service can guarantee 100% impenetrable security, we continuously review and maintain commercially acceptable protections for your personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates or regulatory requirements. Any material changes will be reflected in the updated date above and communicated in the application where appropriate. Continued use of AxomQuest signifies acceptance of the updated terms.
