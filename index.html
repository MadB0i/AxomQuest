Straight answers.

The questions every aspirant asks before installing.

Are the mock tests really free?

Yes. Mocks 001–030 and Daily 10 practice are completely free and supported by ads. To practice premium mocks (031–108), an optional AxomQuest Pro subscription is available.

What does AxomQuest Pro unlock?

AxomQuest Pro unlocks premium mocks 031 through 108 and provides an ad-free practice experience. It is an auto-renewing monthly subscription at ₹99/month through Google Play that can be cancelled anytime.

Which languages does the app support?

English, Hindi and Assamese. Any question can be translated instantly in the middle of a test, and your language preference is saved.

Will the app work on my phone?

The app runs on Android 8.0 and newer and is optimized for budget and mid-range devices. An internet connection is needed to start a test; your completed attempts, scores and bookmarks are stored securely on your device.

Is AxomQuest official or affiliated with any government or defence organisation?

No. AxomQuest is an independent, privately developed app with no affiliation to, or endorsement by, any government or defence organisation. Read the full disclosure.

What happens to my data?

You can delete your account in the app under Account → Delete account, or request deletion by email without reinstalling the app. Cloud account records and entitlement projections are permanently deleted; minimal pseudonymous purchase integrity records remain for fraud prevention. Follow Delete your AxomQuest account for details.

What about historical Mission Khaki purchases?

Existing users who previously purchased the historical one-time Mission Khaki Lifetime Pro package retain full access to premium mock tests without paying a monthly subscription.
